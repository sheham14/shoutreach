These become the From: line in the inbox — From Name <From Email> — and From Email is where replies go. It does not have to match the SMTP username below; set them differently to send as an alias.
SMTP — Outbound
The mailbox you authenticate as — usually the full address.
With two-factor auth on, this must be an app-specific password, not your login password. They are per-mailbox — a new account needs its own.
