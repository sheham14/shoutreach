The problem

Cold outreach SaaS is broken.
Here's why.

💸
You're renting, not owning
Instantly starts at $97/month. Smartlead at $94. Apollo at $99. They can hike prices, change plans, or shut down — and your entire outreach stack goes with them.
🔒
Your data lives on their servers
Every contact, reply, and campaign lives on someone else's infrastructure. Export limits, account bans, or a billing hiccup can lock you out of your own business overnight.
🤖
Locked into their AI at their markup
When platforms add "AI features," they proxy your requests and charge a premium on top. You don't choose the model, the provider, or the cost. You just pay their rate.
Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

⚡
Multi-Inbox Rotation
Assign multiple SMTP accounts per campaign. Sends rotate round-robin automatically — no manual switching, no warm-up conflicts.
{}
Campaign Variables
Define {{location}} once per campaign. Run identical sequences across Toronto, Brampton, Mississauga — one template, unlimited cities.
Unique to ShoutReach
✓
MX Validation + Sales Signal
Every imported email is validated against live DNS. Invalid emails aren't discarded — they're flagged: this business has outdated tech, and that's a door opener.
Unique to ShoutReach
🕐
Timezone-Aware Sending
Set a timezone per campaign. 9–5 sends land during local business hours — not at 3am because your VPS happens to be in UTC.
◈
A/B Variant Testing
Weighted split testing per step. Contacts are assigned variants at enrollment. Reply rates tracked side-by-side so you know what's actually working.
↩
Smart Reply Detection
IMAP scans every 5 minutes. Distinguishes real replies from OOO auto-replies using RFC 3834 headers — sequences only stop for genuine responses.
✦
AI Copy Review (BYOK)
Score, critique, and rewrite emails with Claude, Gemini, or GPT. Connect your own API key — no markup, no middleman, no vendor lock-in.
🔍
Google Maps Scraper
Scrape businesses by niche and city. Emails, websites, addresses — imported directly into your contacts with MX validation on the way in.
